C. V. Raman

Personal Information

Full Name: Chandrasekhara Venkata Raman

Born: 7 November 1888, Tiruchirappalli, British India

Died: 21 November 1970, Bangalore, India

Nationality: Indian

Field: Physics

Biography

C. V. Raman was an Indian physicist famous for his work on light scattering. He discovered the Raman Effect and was awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1930. He was the first Asian to receive a Nobel Prize in any branch of science.

Raman Effect

The Raman Effect is a change in the wavelength of light that occurs when a light beam is deflected by molecules. It was a major breakthrough in optical physics.

Achievements

  • Nobel Prize in Physics (1930)
  • Bharat Ratna (1954)
  • Founded Raman Research Institute, Bangalore

Legacy

India celebrates National Science Day on 28 February to mark his discovery of the Raman Effect. His work inspired generations of Indian scientists.
